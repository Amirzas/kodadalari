FAQ

Where is progress stored? Only on your device. There is no account; deleting the app deletes the progress. Restoring a device backup on a new device carries it over.

Does it need internet? No. The app works offline.

Does it run real Python? No. In the "first step to Python" lessons you read, complete and write short Python snippets, which the app's own limited interpreter uses to move Robo.
